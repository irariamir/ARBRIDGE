FileMind · Telegram

Your files,
where you chat.

The grammY bot supports private-chat pairing, illustrative subscription plans, manual receipt submissions, reviewer callbacks, and recent delivery status in this development preview.

Connect the extension

  1. Press Start in the configured bot.
  2. In the extension choose Connect Telegram and send the displayed /connect code to the bot.
  3. Enter the one-time confirmation code the bot replies with. Pairing codes expire after five minutes.

Plans and payment review

Example prices are $4.99/month and $49.99/year. They are not a public offer. Payments are disabled until the operator configures a legitimate payment method and private review group. When enabled, users may submit a receipt photo or PDF; authorized numeric Telegram IDs can approve or reject it in the group. The operator can also use the protected /admin page.

Manual transfer status is not payment verification. The reviewer must independently confirm the transfer. No payment processor is integrated, and subscriptions are temporary in-memory records.

Transfer a public document

After pairing and Telegram pairing, the extension server-side downloads a public HTTPS link and sends the validated file using Telegram sendDocument. Telegram must first be started by the user. The bot cannot access files behind your browser login, and files are not stored in a permanent ARBRIDGE library.

Still planned

Incoming Telegram file ingestion, document extraction, AI summaries, semantic search, durable user accounts, and synchronized deletion are not implemented yet.
