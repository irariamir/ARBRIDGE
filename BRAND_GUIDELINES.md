# ARBRIDGE brand guidelines

## Identity

- **Product name:** ARBRIDGE
- **Company:** ARIAMIR
- **Tagline:** Your digital workspace, connected everywhere.
- **Descriptor:** A browser-to-Telegram document workspace.

The product name should remain ARBRIDGE, not BridgeMind. ARBRIDGE is the existing repository and requested project name; ARIAMIR is the company. Use uppercase in the wordmark/header and normal title case in body copy. Never imply ARBRIDGE is an official product of Google or Telegram.

## Voice

Be practical, transparent, and respectful of user control. Say what a transfer does, where the content goes, and whether work is still pending. Avoid exaggerated AI claims, fake performance guarantees, fabricated endorsements, and vague success messages.

## Visual cues

Use the ARIAMIR reference palette: green action accents (`#006239`, `#3ECF8E`), charcoal text, neutral paper, and thin dividers. Do not reproduce or alter the ARIAMIR mark to create an ARBRIDGE logo. Build a simple typographic ARBRIDGE wordmark until an approved product mark exists.

## Language

Product-facing copy is natural English. Translate all controls, errors, help, and empty states before adding additional locales. No mixed-language UI.

## Prohibited effects

No shadows of any kind. No glassmorphism, generic AI purple/blue gradients, over-rounded card grids, fake customer claims, or inaccessible low-contrast status colors.
